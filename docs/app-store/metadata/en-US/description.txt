Build a focused practice routine for the CFOP method. CFOP Trainer helps you organize F2L, OLL and PLL algorithms, recognize cases, and track your execution times.

• Browse 41 F2L, 57 OLL and 21 PLL cases.
• Save your preferred algorithms and choose a favorite for each case.
• Explore cube diagrams and interactive 3D previews.
• Practice selected cases in a random order, with optional random AUF.
• Use standard or inverse practice modes.
• Record times and compare your best results and averages.
• Save case selections as presets for focused practice sessions.
• Import and export your algorithms.
• Choose a light or dark appearance, in English or Japanese.

Start without an account and practice offline with data stored on your device. Create an optional email/password account to save your settings, algorithms and times online and use them across devices. An internet connection is required to update account data.

The app is designed for use alongside a physical 3×3 cube. CFOP Trainer is an independent training app.
