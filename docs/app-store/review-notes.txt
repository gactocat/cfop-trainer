CFOP Trainer is a practice tool for a physical 3x3 cube. All algorithm reference, 3D previews, random drills, timers and local records work without signing in. No ads, purchases or subscriptions.

Optional account access: Settings on the top page > Account (tap the back button at the header's top left to return to the top page). Use the supplied review credentials; email is already confirmed and no additional verification is needed. On first sign-in, choose Start fresh or import device data. Signed-in changes require internet access and are stored in Supabase. Guest practice works offline.

To delete an account: Settings > Account > Delete account, enter the current password, then confirm permanent deletion. This deletes the authentication account and associated server data. Please use a separate account for deletion testing if you need to retain the supplied review account.

The settings on the top page switch between English and Japanese and between light and dark.

Support and privacy policy links are accessible from Account without signing in. This is an independent cube training app.
